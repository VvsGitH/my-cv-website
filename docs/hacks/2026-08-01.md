# Hacks — audit of 2026-08-01

Workarounds currently in the tree. Each exists because a platform, a tool or a
test does not behave the way the straightforward code would assume. None is a
bug today; all of them are code that has to be defended rather than read.

Logged during the documentation cleanup, so the comments defending them could be
cut to one line without losing the record. **This is a to-do list for a later
corrective pass, not documentation** — a hack that gets removed should be struck
from here, and a hack that turns out to be permanent should graduate into an ADR.

Format per entry: what the platform does · what we do instead · what it would
take to remove.

---

## 7. The capture script's viewport and image-decode dance

`scripts/render-captures.mjs`

Two workarounds for silent capture corruption, both fully described in ADR-0009:
the viewport must be set wide enough for the whole Sheet *before* `goto` — 856px,
the 840px paper and its two 8px gutters — or two embedded fonts are never
requested; and every image must be `decode()`d explicitly because Astro's
`<Image>` is `loading="lazy"` and the print pass outruns the network.

**Remove when:** `page.pdf()` can be told to request every declared face, and the
portrait is `loading="eager"` as a page decision.

## 8. The bare `@layer` statement must precede the `@import`s

`src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` — **verify this is still live**

Placed after the imports, the production CSS minifier hoists the layer statement
in a way that reorders effective layer precedence. The failure is
**minifier-only**: dev looks correct and the built site does not.

`src/styles/global.css` appears to carry no bare statement at all, so the first
job here is to establish whether the hazard is currently present or already
sidestepped.

**Remove when:** the minifier stops reordering, or the layer order is expressed
somewhere it cannot hoist.

## 9. The column-prefix specificity workaround

`src/components/structure/Sheet.astro`

A rule needs its column class as a prefix purely to win a specificity contest
against a scoped rule, because Astro's scoping adds an attribute selector.
Without the prefix the first section gains an unwanted gap.

**Remove when:** the two rules stop overlapping, or the gap moves to the
component that owns it.

## 10. `@media (hover: hover)` gating on the Toolbar's controls

`src/components/chrome/toolbar.css`

A tap leaves `:hover` stuck on the control a phone visitor last touched, which
reads as a selected state it is not.

**The gate reaches three of the seven controls, and that is deliberate.** It is
`:is(.toolbar-mode, .toolbar-button):hover`, so the Mode, the download and the
share take a fill and the two language links and two theme radios take none. The
fill is `--color-aside-accent`, which is *exactly* the pill's own fill, so a
hovered half of either pair would paint the selected state onto an unselected
control — the one distinction the pill's border exists to keep legible
(ADR-0025). Do not "complete" the selector.

**Remove when:** never realistically — this is the standard remedy. Listed
because it is a platform wart, not because it is wrong.

## 11. `devtools: true` to make hydration mismatches visible

`astro.config.mjs`

A Preact hydration mismatch stops hydrating and re-renders **in silence** —
Preact logs nothing unless `preact/debug` is loaded. The integration runs with
`devtools: true` purely so the failure is observable.

**Remove when:** Preact warns on mismatch by default.

## 12. Two carve-outs in `toolbar.css` that look redundant with `reset.css`

`src/components/chrome/toolbar.css` — `box-sizing: border-box` on
`.toolbar-switch::before`, and the local `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`
block under it.

**`reset.css` reaches no pseudo-element at all.** `:is()`/`:where()` cannot contain
one and their selector lists are *forgiving*, so both
`:where(*, *::before, *::after)` (the `box-sizing` rule) and
`:where(*, *::before, *::after, *::backdrop)` (the reduced-motion rule) parse down to
`:where(*)`. The bar is the first thing in this tree to need either on a `::before`,
and it paid for both: the pill computed `content-box`, so `50%` plus a 1px border
overhung its group by **4.00px**, and its 260ms travel ran in full for a reader who
had asked for stillness (measured `0.26s` against a control element's `1e-05s`).

Both local rules are therefore **load-bearing, not belt-and-braces**. `reset.css`'s own
comment — *"`::backdrop` is a descendant of nothing, so `*` does not reach it"* —
records the belief that the list works, and is false.

**Remove when:** `reset.css` is fixed to name pseudo-elements in their own rule.
Tracked as `docs/issues/reset-pseudo-elements/`.

## 13. The language's travel is asserted on the **departing** document

`tests/toolbar.spec.ts` — *the language's travel*

Headless Chromium **skips** the arriving side of the cross-document View Transition:
`pagereveal` fires with nothing to inspect and the console reports
`Transition was skipped`, while a headed run performs it normally. There is nothing
deterministic to assert on the page that lands.

So the suite asserts the half that is deterministic: a `pageswap` listener on the
**departing** document records whether the event carried a `ViewTransition`, and the
test reads that back after `waitForURL`. That is the document's own opt-in
(`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`) proved to have taken effect, which is the
claim worth pinning; the arriving animation is the platform's to run.

**Remove when:** headless Chromium performs cross-document transitions. Do not
"improve" this into an assertion on the arriving page in the meantime — it will pass
vacuously.

## 14. The reduced-motion opt-out for the cross-document transition has no test

`src/styles/reset.css` — `@view-transition { navigation: none }` inside the
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block.

**It is not assertable, and this was established rather than assumed.** A test was
written for it, and then **proved vacuous by deleting `navigation: none` from the
built CSS and watching it still pass** — under `emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })`
the arriving side is skipped anyway (see 13), so the observable outcome is identical
with the rule and without it. The test was removed rather than left standing as
false assurance.

The rule stays, because it is the correct and only way to opt a cross-document
transition out (a JavaScript gate cannot reach one; ADR-0016 gates the *same-document*
theme circle in JavaScript for the mirror-image reason). It is simply unguarded.

**Remove when:** headless Chromium performs cross-document transitions, at which point
13 and 14 close together.

## Removed

Struck from the list once the hack itself was gone. The surviving entries keep
their original numbers — `Sheet.astro` cites §9 by number — so the sequence has gaps rather than being renumbered.

- **5. The font wait has to come *after* hydration in the test helper** and
  **6. `--header-height` is a hard-coded manual measurement.** Both went at once:
  6 was the stated dependency of 5. Main's header Block now takes
  `min-block-size: var(--photo-size)` instead of the two columns being reconciled
  by arithmetic over a hand measurement, so the header's rendered height positions
  nothing and a font-metrics wobble during hydration cannot move Main's first
  heading. `tests/support/page.ts` loads the faces before hydration again.
  ADR-0011 carries the decision and what it costs.

- **1. `.drawer:not([open]) { display: none }` must beat the UA stylesheet**,
  **2. `::-webkit-scrollbar` duplicated beside `scrollbar-width`/`scrollbar-color`**,
  **3. The scroll lock `showModal()` does not apply** and
  **4. The `matchMedia` guard closes the panel directly, bypassing the signal.** All four
  at once: they were `drawer.css` and `Drawer.tsx`, and ADR-0017 deleted the Drawer with
  the width that made it necessary. Nothing replaced them — the Aside's Blocks read in the
  reading column now, so there is no panel to open, lock scroll behind, or close on a media
  change. The measurements they carry are ADR-0008's and stay there; struck here late,
  during the ADR-0025 record.
